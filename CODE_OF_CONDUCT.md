# Community conduct

OSCAR welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds
and experience levels. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, and other spaces maintained for this project.

- Treat people respectfully. Critique code and ideas without personal attacks.
- Give actionable feedback and make room for questions and good-faith disagreement.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or post sexualized or abusive content.
- Do not disclose another person's private information without permission.
- Respect requests to stop disruptive behavior and follow maintainer moderation.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, limit participation, or
exclude participants when necessary. Responses should be proportionate to the
behavior and consider the safety and privacy of affected people.

## Reporting concerns

Contact a repository maintainer privately using a contact method they publish on
their GitHub profile. If no private method is available, open a minimal issue asking
for a private conduct-reporting channel. Do not include names, private messages,
or incident details in that public request. If the concern involves a maintainer,
contact another maintainer when one is available.

Include the relevant project location, what happened, and any evidence you can
share safely. Reports should be handled with discretion, but absolute
confidentiality and response deadlines cannot be guaranteed.
